# AHC Non-Custodial Payment Policy

## Product boundary

Affordable Housing Cameroon records and reconciles only **AHC service payments**: Agent Access, Listing Passes, featured landmark pins, and field-verification services. It does not receive, route, store, split, or promise to safeguard rent, tenancy deposits, viewing payments, agency commissions outside its own declared service fees, or any other owner–tenant settlement money.

> AHC's Total Move-In Cash Required is a transparency disclosure. It is not a request for payment to AHC and does not make AHC a party to the tenancy transaction.

## Operational controls

| Control | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Public disclosure | Property details must state that AHC is not an escrow, rent-collection, or deposit-holding service. |
| Service-fee receipts | Only reconciled AHC service orders receive official AHC receipt codes. An official receipt proves only that specific AHC service payment. |
| Rent and deposit safety | Seekers should inspect the home, agree terms in writing, and obtain the appropriate counterparty receipt before paying a landlord or agent. |
| Fraud reporting | A request for an unofficial AHC fee must be reported through the listing-report workflow. The report is investigated under the fair-review controls. |
| Staff prohibition | Staff must not accept tenancy money, advertise an escrow arrangement, promise release of rent/deposits, or describe the administrative ledger as a payment wallet. |

## Future partner-mediated payments

AHC must not add a custom escrow, wallet, rent-collection, deposit-holding, or settlement feature. Any future mediated payment concept requires all of the following before development or public launch:

1. Written owner approval defining the exact payment scope and customer journey.
2. A legal and regulatory review appropriate to Cameroon and the intended payment rails.
3. A duly approved payment partner whose agreement explicitly covers the proposed flow.
4. A documented data-protection, dispute, refund, reconciliation, and incident-response design.
5. Separate security review, audit trail, user disclosures, and launch approval.

Until those gates are complete, the application must preserve the non-custodial boundary above.
