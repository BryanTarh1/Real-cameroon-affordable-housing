# Seeker Account Privacy Research Notes

## Sources reviewed on 18 August 2026

1. The Presidency of the Republic of Cameroon publishes **Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 relating to personal data protection in Cameroon**. The official publication page is available at <https://prc.cm/en/multimedia/documents/10271-law-n-2024-017-of-23-12-2024-web>.
2. A Cameroon-focused legal analysis by D. Moukouri & Partners/Lex Africa reports that the law gave organisations collecting, storing, adapting, transferring, or erasing personal data until **23 June 2026** to align their processing protocols. It highlights consent, disclosure, transfer controls, governance, privacy-by-design, processing records, and the value of a qualified DPO for large-scale processing: <https://lexafrica.com/2025/10/cameroon-data-protection-law-compliance/>.
3. A Cameroon legal analysis by CLG Global describes explicit, informed, specific, freely given, and revocable consent; data minimisation; rights of access, correction, erasure, and objection; and conditions for cross-border transfers under Law No. 2024/017: <https://clgglobal.com/understanding-cameroons-personal-data-protection-act-opportunities-challenges-and-compliance-recommendation/>.

## Implications for AHC

AHC should treat names, email addresses, phone numbers, saved homes, browsing history, viewing requests, alert preferences, device/session data, and approximate location preferences as personal data. It should collect only the data needed to provide the requested marketplace feature, separate essential-account processing from optional marketing and behavioural analytics, offer practical account controls, document vendors and foreign hosting locations, and obtain Cameroon-qualified legal advice before declaring full compliance or deciding whether authority formalities or a DPO are mandatory.
