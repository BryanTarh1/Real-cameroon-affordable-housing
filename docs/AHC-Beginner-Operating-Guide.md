# Affordable Housing Cameroon: Simple Operating Guide

**Version:** August 2026  
**Audience:** First-time visitors, Seekers, Agents, Field Moderators, and Admins  
**Purpose:** To help a new person use AHC safely and understand what the platform does—and does not—handle.

> **The main rule:** AHC helps people find and verify rental homes. It does **not** hold, collect, or guarantee rent, deposits, viewing money, agent commissions, or any agreement between a tenant and an owner.

## 1. Start here

Open the AHC website and use the home search. You can browse homes without an account. Search by city, neighbourhood, landmark, monthly rent, or the maximum **Total Move-In Cash Required** you can afford. This total is more important than rent alone because it combines the declared advance, deposit, agency fee, service fee, and first-month utilities.

The map intentionally shows an approximate landmark area rather than the exact compound door. This protects the Agent and the property from people bypassing the arranged contact process. A fresh listing normally shows a reconfirmation date; listings that are not reconfirmed within 14 days are removed from public search.

| If you are… | Start at | What you can do first |
|---|---|---|
| A person looking for a home | Home page | Search homes and compare Total Move-In Cash Required |
| An Agent or supplier | `/#/agent` | Sign in, set up a profile, pay for AHC platform access, and submit listings |
| A Field Moderator | `/#/operations` | Sign in to claim and complete field verification work |
| An Admin | `/#/admin` | Sign in to manage AHC governance, service orders, and staff controls |

## 2. How a Seeker finds a home

First, use the filters to keep results within your real cash limit. Open a listing only when its total cost, location area, and number of rooms look suitable. AHC asks you to sign in when you open a property detail because actions such as watching an approved Walk-Thru, reporting a problem, requesting a viewing, or starting contact need an accountable user.

On the property page, read every item in the cost breakdown. Check the freshness note, physical-verification badge, neighbourhood badges, and any approved Walk-Thru video. A verified video is a field-recorded view of the home; it is useful evidence, but you should still inspect the home before paying anyone.

Use **Chat on WhatsApp** only when you are ready to ask about that specific home. The button records the AHC contact intent and opens a prepared WhatsApp message. It does not mean AHC has seen or guaranteed the messages, a viewing, a payment, or a tenancy.

| Seeker action | What to check | When to report |
|---|---|---|
| Compare costs | The total includes every declared upfront item | A new, undeclared fee is demanded |
| Watch a Walk-Thru | The video matches the listing details | The actual home differs materially |
| Arrange a viewing | Confirm the time and meet safely | The home is unavailable or suspicious |
| Pay for a rental | Deal directly with the responsible parties after your own checks | Never send tenancy money to AHC as a rental intermediary |

If an Agent asks for more money than the published, itemised amount, use the report option in the signed-in listing. Give clear facts: what was asked, when, and which cost was different. AHC may place the listing on a safety hold while the report is reviewed. A report starts an investigation; it is not automatic proof against an Agent.

## 3. How an Agent adds a property

Open `/#/agent` and sign in with your AHC account. If it is your first visit, create your public profile with the name and WhatsApp number you want seekers to use. All property suppliers follow this same Agent path; there is no separate Owner workspace.

AHC is paid from the beginning. Before a commercial listing can enter review, create the correct AHC service order, pay using the official merchant instruction, and submit the provider transaction reference. An Admin checks the evidence before the service is confirmed. Do not treat submission of a reference as approval.

When the Admin confirms an AHC service, an official receipt code is issued. Open **Orders and your inventory**, select **Print receipt**, and save or print it. The receipt is only for the named AHC platform service, such as Agent Access, a Listing Pass, a Featured Landmark Pin, or physical verification. It is not a rent receipt.

Then enter the property carefully. Include the real costs, public landmark, available date, map privacy radius, and all required listing information. The first submission goes to independent review. It is not public until the required paid access and Listing Pass are active, the listing is reviewed, and the required status is approved.

After publication, reconfirm that the home remains available before the 14-day freshness period ends. The inventory card shows the last reconfirmation date and the remaining days. If the home is no longer available, do not reconfirm it.

## 4. How a Field Moderator works

Open `/#/operations` and sign in with the account given to you by AHC. A Field Moderator account is created by trusted staff; it is not available through public registration.

Your job is to verify a property on the ground. Claim a suitable verification assignment, visit the landmark area, and record the required evidence. The evidence must show whether the home matches the Agent’s photos and declared information. Record honest observations and the neighbourhood information requested by the form. Do not invent proof, reuse old photos, or approve a home you did not visit.

Some completed checks receive an independent second visit. Cooperate with this process. Your commission status is visible in Operations, but payment is never automatic. It remains held until the required proof is stored, any selected second visit is resolved, and an Admin reviews the evidence. A Field Moderator does **not** reconcile platform payment references, approve supplier receipts, handle rent, or approve commission payout.

## 5. How an Admin governs the platform

Open `/#/admin` and sign in. Admin access is private and should be assigned only to trusted staff. The Admin workspace includes commercial settings, official service-order reconciliation, cash-flow audit, verification commission controls, trust reports, and role management.

Use the service-order queue only for AHC platform services. Match the payment provider evidence to the AHC order before selecting **Confirm platform service**. A confirmed order may activate an AHC service and issue an official receipt. Use the receipt preview to print or save the record. Reject a reference when it cannot be independently matched, and record a clear reason.

The commission ledger is separate from service-order reconciliation. Review the stored field evidence and any selected independent audit before approving a held Field Moderator allocation as payable. Do not promise or record a payout before that evidence review is complete.

When reviewing trust reports, remember that a safety hold is not a final decision. Check the listing facts, the report pattern, and both parties’ information before applying a lasting account restriction. Use trusted-role controls carefully: public registration must never create a Moderator or Admin.

## 6. Everyday safety checklist

| Before you act | Safe practice |
|---|---|
| Before visiting a home | Read the full cost breakdown, use the public landmark only, and arrange contact through the listing |
| Before sending money for a tenancy | Visit or verify independently; AHC is not an escrow or rent-collection service |
| Before accepting a platform receipt | Check that it names an AHC platform service and has an AHC receipt code |
| Before uploading evidence | Confirm it is your own current visit evidence and does not expose unnecessary private information |
| Before changing a staff role | Confirm the person is trusted, the account exists, and the action is necessary |

## 7. If something goes wrong

If a page does not open, first check that you used the full route beginning with `/#/`. Use the visible **Log out** control before switching test roles in the same browser profile. To use more than one account on one device, use separate browser profiles or a private window.

If a listing is inaccurate, use the report option from its signed-in detail page. If an Agent cannot submit a listing, check that Agent Access and a Listing Pass are both confirmed. If a Field Moderator cannot see an assignment, it may be unassigned, not yet route-ready, or already claimed. If an Admin cannot see a control, stop and check that the account has the Admin role rather than trying to bypass the route.

> **Remember:** The platform is designed to make rental information clearer and field checks more accountable. It does not replace your own inspection, agreement, legal advice, or safe-payment judgment.
