# Affordable Housing Cameroon — 90-Day Launch Budget

## Important planning note

I am not a licensed financial advisor; this is planning analysis, not guaranteed financial advice. Actual costs should be validated with local quotes in Yaoundé and Douala before you commit funds. The figures below are **indicative planning ranges in FCFA**, using **600 XAF = 1 USD only as a simple budgeting conversion**, not as a live exchange-rate quotation.

## Bottom line

For a credible first 90-day pilot, I would plan to raise or reserve approximately **38,000,000 XAF**, or about **US$54,000 at the illustrative planning rate**. This is the recommended “base” budget for building a real MVP, creating an initial verified catalogue, operating moderation and support, and learning in selected neighborhoods in Yaoundé and Douala.

You can attempt a very lean validation for approximately **19,300,000 XAF**, but that version requires substantial founder time, a smaller catalogue, limited design and engineering scope, and more manual operations. A stronger two-city launch with more staff, more supply acquisition, and a larger marketing effort may require approximately **73,000,000 XAF**.

| Scenario | Purpose | 90-day budget | Approx. USD at 600 XAF/USD |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Lean validation | Prove the problem and test a small catalogue | **19.3M XAF** | **$32.2K** |
| Recommended pilot | Build and operate a credible two-city MVP | **38.0M XAF** | **$54.0K** |
| Growth launch | Larger catalogue, stronger team, broader acquisition | **73.0M XAF** | **$113.3K** |

## Recommended base budget: 38,000,000 XAF

This is the budget I would use for planning because it is large enough to avoid launching with a weak catalogue or no moderation, while still being disciplined and pilot-sized.

| Budget area | 90-day amount | What it covers |
|---|---:|---|
| Discovery and validation | **2,500,000 XAF** | Interviews, transport, participant appreciation, research coordination, synthesis |
| Product and MVP development | **15,000,000 XAF** | UX, frontend, backend, admin moderation tools, QA, deployment preparation |
| Hosting, software, communications | **1,200,000 XAF** | Hosting, domains, monitoring, messaging, collaboration, storage, testing tools |
| Catalogue acquisition and verification | **4,800,000 XAF** | Field visits, listing intake, photography/data capture, phone verification, partner onboarding |
| Moderation and customer support | **4,500,000 XAF** | Part-time moderators, support coverage, incident handling, quality reviews |
| Community and pilot marketing | **3,500,000 XAF** | Local partnerships, targeted outreach, printed materials, small test campaigns |
| Legal, privacy, accounting, administration | **1,500,000 XAF** | Local legal review, privacy documentation, contracts, accounting and registration support |
| Contingency reserve | **5,000,000 XAF** | Approximately 15% reserve for rework, fraud response, field cost increases, or delays |
| **Total** | **38,000,000 XAF** | **Recommended 90-day pilot reserve** |

## How the cash should be released

Do not spend the entire amount on development at the beginning. Release funds through decision gates so that weak assumptions are discovered before the budget is exhausted.

| Release stage | Timing | Amount | Release condition |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Stage 1 — discovery | Days 1–20 | **4.0M XAF** | Target segment, user problem, pilot neighborhoods, and risk map are validated |
| Stage 2 — supply and prototype | Days 21–45 | **9.0M XAF** | Initial partners recruited, prototype tested, and verification SOP approved |
| Stage 3 — MVP and catalogue | Days 46–70 | **13.0M XAF** | Core flows work, catalogue is being verified, and moderation is staffed |
| Stage 4 — controlled pilot | Days 71–90 | **7.0M XAF** | Pilot dashboard is active and support operations can handle incidents |
| Reserve held back | Throughout | **5.0M XAF** | Released only for documented risks or validated opportunities |
| **Total** |  | **38.0M XAF** |  |

| Lightweight MVP or prototype build | **8,500,000 XAF** |

The lean version should not attempt a national marketplace, integrated payments, complex AI recommendations, or a large paid advertising campaign. Its purpose is to establish evidence that people will use the service and that verified supply can be maintained.

## Monthly operating budget after launch

After the initial 90-day build and pilot, the base operation should be expected to cost approximately **6,000,000–10,000,000 XAF per month**, depending on staffing and catalogue coverage.

| Recurring area | Estimated monthly range |
|---|---:|
| Moderation and customer support | **2.0M–3.5M XAF** |
| Field verification and catalogue freshness | **1.2M–2.5M XAF** |
| Engineering and product maintenance | **1.5M–2.5M XAF** |
| Hosting, messaging, storage, and software | **0.3M–0.8M XAF** |
| Partnerships and community acquisition | **0.8M–1.5M XAF** |
| Accounting, legal, and administration | **0.2M–0.5M XAF** |
| **Estimated total** | **6.0M–10.8M XAF/month** |

This excludes salaries for founders unless they are included in the operating plan. It also excludes office rent, vehicle purchase, loans, taxes that depend on the final legal structure, payment-processing costs, and any large-scale advertising campaign.

## Recommended team assumptions

The base budget assumes a small, focused team rather than a large permanent organization.

| Role | Suggested model during pilot |
|---|---|
| Product/founder lead | Founder-led or one full-time product owner |
| Engineering | Small local or mixed team with clear fixed deliverables |
| Field/cataIogue lead | One accountable person coordinating city operations |
| Moderators | Part-time coverage with escalation responsibility |
| Research and partnerships | Short contracts or founder-led during discovery |
| Legal/accounting | External professional support rather than full-time hires |

Local quotes should be collected for every role. The purpose of this table is to structure the budget, not to establish a wage scale.

## Biggest budget risks

The largest risk is usually not hosting. It is the cost of acquiring, verifying, and keeping listings current. A marketplace can spend heavily on development and still fail because the data is stale or contacts do not respond.

The second risk is uncontrolled scope. Payments, native mobile apps, advanced recommendations, nationwide coverage, document verification, and complex mapping can each consume substantial time without proving the initial housing journey.

The third risk is launching too early with insufficient support. Reports, scams, duplicate listings, wrong directions, and unavailable homes create reputational damage if there is no one responsible for resolving them.

## Where to save money safely

Save by using a mobile-first web app before building native mobile applications, by using list and landmark-based discovery before advanced map functionality, by keeping the first catalogue small, by using external legal and accounting support, and by operating a human-in-the-loop trust process before attempting automated fraud detection.

Do not save money by removing moderation, hiding missing cost information, launching with unverified listings, collecting sensitive documents without a clear purpose, or spending the contingency reserve on marketing before the contact and visit funnel works.

## My recommendation

Plan around **38,000,000 XAF for the first 90 days**, but commit it in stages. If your available capital is below **20,000,000 XAF**, start with discovery, a manually verified catalogue, and a lightweight prototype rather than promising a full marketplace. If you can secure approximately **38,000,000 XAF**, build the controlled two-city MVP and keep at least **5,000,000 XAF untouched** as a risk reserve.

Before approving the budget, obtain at least three local quotes for development, field verification, moderation, legal review, and marketing. Replace the planning ranges with quoted amounts, then recalculate the reserve and monthly runway.
