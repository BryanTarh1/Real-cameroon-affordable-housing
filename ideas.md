# Design direction — Affordable Housing Cameroon

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Sahel Editorial
Very warm editorial design with paper texture, civic colors, and a field-guide feeling. It treats housing information as a public-interest resource rather than a commercial marketplace.

**Probability:** 0.06

### Theme Name: Signal / Trust Network
A dark, high-contrast interface with bright signal accents and layered data panels. It communicates verification, operational rigor, and a trustworthy housing intelligence system.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Courtyard Atlas
A sunlit, tactile visual system inspired by courtyards, woven textures, neighborhood maps, and painted wayfinding signs. It makes a complex requirements document feel human, navigable, and locally grounded.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen direction: Courtyard Atlas

### Design Movement
Contemporary African editorial modernism: tactile wayfinding, civic cartography, and warm material surfaces translated into a premium digital product brief.

### Core Principles
1. **Human before institutional.** Use a grounded, welcoming interface that speaks to people searching for a home, not only to product teams.
2. **Trust is visible.** Verification, affordability, and risk should be represented with distinct, legible visual signals.
3. **Information has a route.** The page should feel like a guided atlas with a persistent index and strong section landmarks, not a flat stack of cards.
4. **Local texture, modern execution.** Use warm earth tones, ink-like navy, leaf green, and hand-drawn map motifs without becoming folkloric or decorative for its own sake.

### Color Philosophy
The signature color is **Mango Ochre**: a confident, ownable orange-gold associated with sunlight, visibility, and practical action. It is paired with deep ink navy for reliability, muted clay for contextual warmth, and leaf green for verified / viable states. Backgrounds are off-white rather than pure white to create a printed-field-guide feel and reduce glare.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an editorial atlas structure: a narrow sticky chapter rail on desktop, full-width hero with an offset information panel, section headers that act like map markers, and asymmetric two-column modules. Avoid a generic centered marketing grid. Long-form content is broken into readable “routes” with section numbering, side notes, and compact comparison tables.

### Signature Elements
- A recurring **wayfinding tab** with section numbers and short labels.
- A **trust pulse** visual language using outlined circles, check marks, and small status strips.
- A faint **topographic contour pattern** used sparingly behind hero and roadmap areas.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like opening a field guide: navigation anchors reveal where the user is, chips filter or highlight content, and expandable cards expose deeper detail without forcing a page transition. Buttons should be direct and practical, with microcopy that tells the user what will happen.

### Animation
Use short, grounded transitions: section markers slide 4–8px into place, cards lift subtly on hover, and the hero status panel uses a slow pulse only for the “trust” indicator. Respect reduced motion. Avoid bouncing, excessive parallax, and decorative motion unrelated to comprehension.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for major editorial headlines and **Manrope** for body copy, labels, navigation, and data. Headline scale should be expressive but compact; body copy should remain calm and highly readable. Use uppercase micro-labels with generous tracking for chapter markers.

### Brand Essence
A practical product blueprint for finding safer, more affordable homes in Cameroon — built for founders, field teams, and communities who need clarity before code. **Grounded, candid, useful.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are specific and human. CTAs are action-oriented but never overpromising. Microcopy names uncertainty instead of hiding it.

Example lines:
- “Make the real cost visible before the first visit.”
- “Start with trust, then scale the catalogue.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a bold symbol based on a simple courtyard plan: four offset blocks forming an open home shape around a central sun dot. The symbol should work alone as an app mark and favicon; the wordmark should use custom-spaced uppercase lettering rather than a default logo font.

### Signature Brand Color
**Mango Ochre — #E5A23C.** It is warm, highly visible, and distinct from the typical blue-green palette of civic technology products.

## Content architecture

The website will present the analysis as a guided product blueprint with these chapters: Overview, Context, Users, Product requirements, Trust and safety, Data and architecture, MVP, Roadmap, Risks, and Launch criteria. A sticky “On this page” rail will make the document navigable, while the hero will summarize the product thesis and the two-city launch recommendation.
